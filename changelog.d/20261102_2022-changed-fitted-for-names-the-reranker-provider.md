- **Breaking, and deliberately in a minor: `fitted_for` now names the reranker's provider, and thresholds without one read `unknown`.** A reranker's scores sit on its provider's own scale (`sentence-transformers`: 0 to 1; `fastembed`: raw logits, -9.28 to 10.31 over 2,556 questions), and `fitted_for` held `model@revision` only, which is the same string under both providers for a manifest that names no revision. So changing `[rerank] provider` kept applying thresholds fitted on the other scale: a result that was `high` became `low` with no warning (measured 20261031 23:43 UTC, and again after the `pnk sync --rebuild` an embedding-provider change requires, 20261102 09:08 UTC), and on 1,140 answerable MultiHop-RAG questions the `sentence-transformers` thresholds labelled 26.7% of `fastembed`'s scores low-confidence where its own labelled 1.3%. **`ModelInfo.fingerprint()` is now `provider:model@revision`** (`provider:model` when `[rerank]` sets no revision), which `python -m pinakes.calibrate` prints and `search` and `pnk doctor` compare by equality, never by parsing. A mismatch is `unknown` in `search`, with the two fingerprints in its reason, and a `FAIL` in `pnk doctor`, exit 1. **Who this breaks: every manifest that carries `[retrieval.confidence]` fitted before the provider was recorded.** Its `fitted_for` has no provider, so it is refused: on this repository's demo KB, this change on its unchanged manifest answered `unknown` for all 74 golden questions (`confidence_coverage` 0.0 against 1.0). It is refused, not guessed, because the only provider to guess from is the current `[rerank] provider`, which is exactly what may have changed. **The fix is one line if you know which provider you fitted under:** `pnk doctor` prints it, `fitted_for = "<provider>:<model>"` (add `@<revision>` if `[rerank]` sets one). If you do not know, re-fit with `python -m pinakes.calibrate <kb>`. The demo KB's manifest carries the new form, and **the golden set is unchanged by it**: the published 0.32.9 on the old manifest and this change on the new one agree on all 74 questions in every field of the per-question rows, with the same labels (54 `high`, 14 `medium`, 6 `low`), `confidence_coverage` 1.0, `false_abstain` 0.0152 and `false_confidence` 0.25, and the repository's own gate reports no regression against its baseline. The `notes` template is `notes@1.3`, comments only: its `fitted_for` hint names the provider and says to paste what `python -m pinakes.calibrate` prints, and its `revision` hint no longer says `pnk doctor` reports the current one or that pinning works under `fastembed`; a KB on `notes@1.2` sees two comment hunks in `pnk upgrade` (the `revision` hint and the `fitted_for` hint; the `[kb] template` line never produces one). `tools/build_rfc_corpus.py` stamps the provider. `docs/MANIFEST.md` and the `calibrate.py` docstring say what `fitted_for` records now, and the `[embedding]` paragraph no longer says the embedding's revision enters `fitted_for` (only the reranker's does). **Two silent traps were closed rather than carried over:** the reproducibility test and gate plant a `fitted_for` over the demo's and would have gone on passing while every question read `unknown`, so the test now asserts the label can move and the gate refuses to go on if it cannot (read in the code, not observed failing in production); and a test that asserted the literal "2 versions archived" at every template bump now asserts at least two. Not run: a provider switch at real size with thresholds fitted by `python -m pinakes.calibrate`.
