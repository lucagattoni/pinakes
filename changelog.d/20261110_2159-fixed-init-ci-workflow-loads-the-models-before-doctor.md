- **The workflow `pnk init --ci` writes no longer fails its health check on a cold model cache.** `pnk doctor` loads models offline and downloads none (0.32.9), and `pnk sync` loads the embedder only when a document needs embedding and the reranker never, so on a fresh runner the workflow's `pnk doctor` step failed on the models (the reranker after a sync that embedded something, both after one that embedded nothing, which is the normal run once the index is restored), because the weights were cached nowhere. The workflow now caches `~/.cache/huggingface` under a key of its own and runs one `pnk search warm-up -k 1 > /dev/null` before the health check, which loads both models online. Measured on an empty model cache: `pnk doctor` exited 1 before that search and 0 after it, with 30 documents and with none, and exited 1 on both models after a sync of 30 unchanged documents. **The workflow installs `pinakes[light,pdf]`, so it needs a manifest that names `fastembed`**: pass `--backend light` to `init`, or edit the manifest. **A workflow written earlier is never updated**, since `init` never overwrites one: the [`pnk init` reference](https://github.com/lucagattoni/pinakes/blob/main/docs/CLI.md#pnk-init) gives the two steps to add.
